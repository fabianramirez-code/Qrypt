# Security Policy

Qrypt is at an early stage and has not been audited. Do not rely on it for real secrets yet.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately, not in a public issue:

1. Open the **Security** tab of this repository.
2. Choose **Report a vulnerability**.

The report stays private between you and the maintainers. Please include the version or commit, what you did, and what happened.

## Scope

The design, its assumptions and its known limits are described in the [threat model](docs/threat-model.md).
